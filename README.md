# 1. Executive Summary


## 1.1 Product Overview

Truvix is an artificial intelligence powered vehicle purchase assistant designed to help users make safer and more informed decisions when purchasing second-hand vehicles.

The platform analyzes vehicle listings, images, market data, historical information, and user preferences to generate a comprehensive vehicle intelligence report.

Truvix does not simply provide vehicle information.

It transforms complex automotive data into a clear purchasing recommendation.

The core question Truvix answers:

"Should I buy this vehicle?"

---

## 1.2 Product Philosophy


The second-hand vehicle market contains significant uncertainty.

Buyers often struggle with:

- overpriced listings,
- hidden damage,
- misleading descriptions,
- unknown vehicle history,
- unrealistic mileage claims,
- expensive ownership costs,
- lack of technical knowledge.


Most users are forced to make a high-value financial decision with incomplete information.


Truvix exists to reduce this uncertainty.

The product philosophy:

"Before spending your money, ask Truvix."

# 4. Problem Statement


## 4.1 Market Problem


Buying a second-hand vehicle is one of the largest financial decisions consumers make.

However, buyers face multiple problems:


## Information Asymmetry

Sellers usually know more about the vehicle than buyers.

Examples:

- previous accidents,
- hidden defects,
- market value,
- maintenance history.


---

## Pricing Uncertainty


Users cannot accurately answer:

"Is this vehicle worth this price?"


Current problems:

- similar vehicles have different prices,
- market changes quickly,
- emotional decisions dominate.


---

## Technical Knowledge Gap


Most buyers cannot evaluate:

- mechanical condition,
- visual damage,
- maintenance requirements,
- ownership costs.


---

## Decision Anxiety


Users experience:

"Ya sorunlu çıkarsa?"

"Fazla para verirsem?"

"Bu araç gerçekten alınır mı?"


---
